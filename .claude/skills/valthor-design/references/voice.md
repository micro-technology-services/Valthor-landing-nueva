# Voice and UI copy

Valthor speaks Rioplatense Spanish with **voseo**, always: "Seleccioná", "Activá", "Tenés", never "Selecciona", "Activa", "Tienes". Short sentences, plain verbs, sentence case. Exception: the public website (valthor.app) uses neutral Spanish because it sells in several countries — but still avoid tuteo forms that sound foreign in Argentina when a neutral wording exists.

## Rules

- **Buttons say what happens**: "Tomar chat", "Mandar plantilla", "Publicar respuesta", "Cargar prestador". Not "Enviar", "Aceptar", "Continuar" alone.
- **One action, one name** across the whole flow: button "Tomar chat" → toast "Tomaste el chat".
- **Name things the way the owner thinks**: "Tu IA", "conversaciones", "turnos", "ventas" — not "agente", "pipeline", "webhook", "workspace", "lead scoring".
- **The AI is "Tu IA"**, the owner is "vos". The AI's work is described in past tense as done work: "Tu IA agendó 3 turnos", not "La IA puede agendar turnos".
- **Empty states direct**: what's missing + what it's for + the button that fills it.
- **Errors** say what happened and how to fix it, without apologizing: "Faltan números. Escribilo con código de área, por ejemplo 11 3138 1205."
- **Te necesita** says who is waiting and why: "Lucía pidió hablar con una persona".
- No emojis in the interface. No "→" after text. No marketing filler ("potenciá", "mágico", "al siguiente nivel", "revolucioná").
- Numbers: Argentine format ($16.990, 2.935, 0,1 h, 12 %).
- Dates and times: "Hoy", "Ayer", "Sábado 24 de octubre", "17:09".
